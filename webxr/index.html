Photosensitivity warning

This app shows flickering markers (6–13 Hz). Flicker in this range can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy. Do not continue if you or anyone watching has a history of seizures or epilepsy. Stop immediately if you feel unwell.

Markers are virtual: they exist only in this headset view. The robot only ever receives goals, never speeds; e-stop, the walker grip and teleop always override.
